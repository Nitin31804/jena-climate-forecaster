BEHIND THE FORECAST

Two approaches.
One changing climate.

01 / SARIMA

The daily rhythm

A seasonal statistical model that uses past temperature and a 24-hour cycle. Its shaded band is a 95% model interval.

02 / TEMPORAL FUSION TRANSFORMER

Learning from context

A neural network using a week of temperature history, rolling averages, and calendar cycles. Its band spans the 5th to 95th predicted quantiles.
